    empaques

    How do you paint what cannot be seen? For a long time I have been thinking about bags and packaging, those generic objects in which anything at all might arrive. Manila envelopes, crumpled plastic bags, self-adhesive seals, barcodes, partial texts, logos. Painted in watercolour on white grounds, these wrappings hold themselves up. I am interested in that instant when an object stops pointing towards something and begins to point to itself. The folds show hands that are no longer there. It is not about the contents, but about the gesture of containing. Each package is a question and, at the same time, an unknown.
